Product Pricing Plans — Internal Reference

Starter Plan
Priced at $19 per user per month, billed annually. Includes up to 5 team
members, 10GB of storage, and email support with a 48-hour response SLA.
API access is not included in the Starter plan.

Growth Plan
Priced at $49 per user per month, billed annually. Includes up to 50 team
members, 250GB of storage, priority chat support with a 4-hour response SLA
during business hours, and read-only API access with a rate limit of 1,000
requests per hour.

Enterprise Plan
Custom pricing, negotiated per contract, typically starting around $120 per
user per month for annual commitments over 200 seats. Includes unlimited
storage, a dedicated customer success manager, full API access with a rate
limit of 50,000 requests per hour, and a 1-hour response SLA backed by a
99.9% uptime guarantee.

Add-Ons
Advanced analytics dashboard: $8 per user per month on Growth or Enterprise.
Single sign-on (SSO) via SAML: included free on Enterprise, $200/month flat
fee on Growth, not available on Starter.

Trial and Cancellation
All plans include a 14-day free trial with no credit card required.
Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing cycle; there
are no partial-month refunds.
