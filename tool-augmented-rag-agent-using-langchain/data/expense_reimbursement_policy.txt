Expense Reimbursement Policy — Internal Use Only

Submission Deadline
All expense reports must be submitted through the Expensify portal within
21 calendar days of the purchase date. Reports submitted after this window
require Finance Director approval and may be delayed by a full pay cycle.

Meal Per Diem
Employees traveling for business receive a daily meal per diem of $65 for
domestic travel and $90 for international travel. Alcohol is not covered
under the per diem and must be itemized separately if included on a receipt.

Approval Thresholds
Expenses under $150 are auto-approved by a direct manager. Expenses between
$150 and $1,000 require manager plus department head approval. Any single
expense over $1,000 requires pre-approval from Finance before the purchase
is made, not after.

Mileage Reimbursement
Personal vehicle use for business purposes is reimbursed at $0.67 per mile,
matching the current standard mileage rate. Commuting between home and the
primary office is not reimbursable.

Client Entertainment
Client meals and entertainment are capped at $120 per person per event and
must include the client's name and company in the expense note. Entertainment
expenses without a documented business purpose will be rejected.
