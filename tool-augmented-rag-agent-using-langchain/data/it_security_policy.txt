IT Security Policy — Internal Use Only

Password Requirements
All employee accounts must use a password of at least 14 characters, including
one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character. Passwords must be
rotated every 90 days. Reusing any of the last 8 passwords is not permitted.

VPN and Remote Access
Employees connecting to internal systems from outside the office network must
use the company VPN (client name: NovaShield VPN). VPN sessions automatically
disconnect after 4 hours of inactivity and require re-authentication via the
Okta mobile app.

Device Encryption
All company-issued laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled (BitLocker
on Windows, FileVault on macOS) before they are allowed to connect to the
internal file server. IT performs a compliance scan every Monday at 6 AM.

Incident Reporting
Any suspected security incident (phishing email, lost device, unauthorized
access) must be reported to security@internal.example.com within 1 hour of
discovery. The Security Response Team's on-call line is +1-800-555-0199.
Failure to report within the window may result in a compliance review.

Software Installation
Employees may not install software from outside the approved IT catalog on
company devices without written approval from the IT Manager. Exceptions are
reviewed on the second Friday of each month.
